Modern Paint
Privacy Policy

Modern Paint is a browser-based bitmap image editor. This policy describes what happens when you use its features.

Personal Data

Modern Paint does not include advertising or analytics. Your drawings are normally kept in browser storage on your device. The hosting provider for the copy of the app you use may process standard request data, such as your IP address, to serve the app.

Third-Party Services

Optional features send data to third parties when you use them:

  • File > Upload to Imgur sends the selected image to Imgur for hosting.
  • When you load an image from a URL, Modern Paint requests it from that address. If that fails, it may try the cors.bridged.cc proxy, the legacy image proxy, or the Internet Archive Wayback Machine.
  • Speech Recognition mode may search Bing Images for clip art based on the drawing command you speak.
  • Multi-User mode uses Firebase for real-time collaboration.

These services have their own privacy policies and terms. Their availability and behavior may change.

Local Storage

Modern Paint stores local backups of your work in browser storage on your device.

You can view and delete stored images with File > Manage Storage.

These backups can help if you close the tab or your computer crashes, but should not be your only backup.

Browser private-browsing modes may prevent data from being retained after the session.

A URL containing #local: represents a local session. Its drawing data is stored in your browser rather than shared through Multi-User mode.

Collaborative Sessions

Multi-User sessions are not private. Anyone with access to a session may be able to view, edit, or delete its contents.

A URL containing #session: represents a collaborative session. Do not use it for sensitive information.
